About us

Built in India. Shipped for clients worldwide.

A full-service software and digital solutions company, working with businesses from local SMEs to multi-city enterprises.

Our story

QivaLabs was born from a simple observation: growing businesses everywhere need reliable, high-quality technology partners who understand their context, their market, their constraints, and their ambitions, not just generic development shops that treat every project as a ticket.

We started with a focus on AI and automation, helping businesses automate repetitive workflows and unlock the value locked in their data. Over time our clients needed more: websites, mobile apps, e-commerce platforms, cloud infrastructure, digital marketing. We built the capability to serve all of it, under one roof, from one team that knows your business.
