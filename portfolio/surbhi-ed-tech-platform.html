Surbhi Ed-Tech Platform

Comprehensive ed-tech platform with course listings, student portals, payment integration, and a demo authentication system. 13 pages built on Next.js 16.

Next.jsEd-TechPaymentsTypeScript

The challenge

An education provider needed a platform that could list and sell courses online, give enrolled students a dedicated portal to track their learning, and handle payments — all as a single coherent product rather than a marketing site bolted onto a separate LMS.

The solution

QivaLabs built a 13-page Next.js 16 / TypeScript platform covering the full journey: public course catalogue and detail pages, a student authentication and portal flow, payment integration for course purchases, and an information architecture designed so new courses can be added without structural rework.

Technology used

Next.js 16TypeScriptPayment gateway integrationAuthentication

QivaLabs' role

Platform architecture and full build.

Outcome

Delivered as a complete 13-page platform spanning marketing, enrollment, and student-portal surfaces.
