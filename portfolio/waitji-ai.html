WaitJI AI

India's first AI ad marketplace that monetises the idle wait time inside developer tools. A full-stack SaaS platform with publisher SDK, advertiser dashboard, and real-time impression delivery.

AI / MLSaaSNext.jsNode.js
Visit live site

The challenge

Developer tools, CLIs, build systems, local dev servers, generate large amounts of idle wait time (installs, builds, test runs) that has no monetisation path today. WaitJI's founders needed a way to turn that idle time into ad inventory without disrupting the developer experience, plus a two-sided platform: an SDK publishers could drop into their tools, and a self-serve dashboard advertisers could use to run campaigns.

The solution

QivaLabs designed and built the full-stack platform end to end: a lightweight publisher SDK for embedding ad slots into CLI/wait-time surfaces, a Next.js advertiser dashboard for campaign creation, budget management, and creative upload, and a Node.js backend handling real-time impression delivery, frequency capping, and event tracking. The system was architected for high request volume with low-latency ad serving, since ad calls sit directly in a developer's wait loop.

Technology used

Next.jsNode.jsPostgreSQLRedis (impression/frequency capping)REST API

QivaLabs' role

Full-stack product build, architecture, SDK, dashboard, backend, and launch.

Outcome

WaitJI AI shipped as a standalone product and now operates independently at waitjiai.in, with its own publisher and advertiser onboarding flows live in market.
