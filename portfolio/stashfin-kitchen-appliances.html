The challenge

Stashfin, a kitchen appliance company in the US, needed a premium website that shows its full line-up and wins the trust of everyday American buyers, without overclaiming or filling the page with invented facts.

The solution

QivaLabs designed and built a premium, poster-style website: a bold typographic hero, a showcase of the full nine-appliance line-up with its own product imagery, and plain-spoken messaging about honest specifications, replaceable parts and warranty cover.

Key features

🍽️

Nine-appliance line-up

Every appliance in the range presented with its own product image.

🖼️

Bold typographic hero

A large, poster-style hero that sets a premium tone from the first screen.

✅

Trust-led messaging

Copy focused on honest specs, replaceable parts and warranty cover.

📱

Responsive layout

Looks right on phones, tablets and desktops.

⚡

Lightweight build

A single, fast page with optimised images.

🌐

Custom domain

Live on Stashfin's own domain, stashfinltd.com.
