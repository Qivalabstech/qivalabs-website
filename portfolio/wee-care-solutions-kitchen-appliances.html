The challenge

Wee Care Solutions, a kitchen appliance brand based in Pottsboro, Texas, needed a complete website that presents its products clearly and builds trust with American buyers: a proper catalogue, detailed product pages, and the support and policy pages customers expect before they buy.

The solution

QivaLabs designed and built a full 16-page website in a graphite, porcelain and orange design system: home, shop, categories, product pages, collections, about, journal, contact, FAQ, warranty, shipping, returns, privacy, terms, search and a 404 page. Product photography was prepared, background-matched and compressed to WebP so the catalogue looks consistent and loads fast, and the support email and contact options run through the whole site.

Key features

🛒

Shop and category pages

A full catalogue with categories and filters across the appliance range.

📄

Product pages

Detailed pages for each appliance with a gallery and key information.

📰

Journal

A content section for guides and brand stories that also supports SEO.

🛡️
