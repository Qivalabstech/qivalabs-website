Qivalabs School Connect

School management software and parent communication app for Indian schools. Attendance, fee management, notices, homework and results on one connected platform.

School Management SoftwareParent AppFee ManagementEd-Tech
Visit School Connect website

The challenge

Most schools still run on a mix of registers, WhatsApp groups, paper circulars and spreadsheets. Attendance, fee collection, notices and results live in different places, parents miss updates, and office staff spend hours on follow-ups and manual reports.

The solution

School Connect is a school ERP and parent communication platform that brings admissions, attendance, fee management, notices, homework and exam results onto one connected system, so school admins, teachers and parents all see the same up-to-date information.

Key features

School owners, principals and administrators of private schools, coaching institutes and K-12 institutions in India that want to move from paper and WhatsApp to a proper school ERP.

📣

Parent communication app

Send notices, circulars, event updates and alerts to parents instantly instead of chasing them on WhatsApp groups.

🗓️

Attendance management

Mark student attendance digitally and keep parents informed, with records ready for reports.

💳

Online fee management

Track fee dues, generate receipts and let parents pay school fees online, with fewer manual reminders.

📝

Homework, timetable & results

Share homework and timetables, and publish exam results and report cards from one place.

🧑‍🏫

Teacher & admin dashboard

One dashboard for the principal, office staff and teachers to manage classes, students and daily school operations.

🔒

Secure, role-based access

Separate access for admins, teachers and parents so each user sees only what they should.

Product design, web development and launch of the School Connect platform and its product website.
