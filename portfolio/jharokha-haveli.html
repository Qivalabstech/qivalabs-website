The challenge

A heritage boutique hotel needed a web presence that matched the character of the property, full-screen photography, a sense of place, while still doing the practical job of a hotel site: clear room information, an easy enquiry/booking path, and content that helps guests plan a visit (local attractions, how to reach the property).

The solution

QivaLabs designed and built a Next.js site with an immersive, full-screen visual layout, room detail pages, a booking enquiry form routed to the property's team, and a local area guide section for SEO and guest planning. The build used Tailwind CSS for a fast, consistent design system and was optimised for mobile, since most travel research and booking enquiries happen on phones.

Technology used

Next.jsTailwind CSSResponsive/mobile-first UXTechnical SEO

QivaLabs' role

End-to-end design and development.

Outcome

Delivered and live as the property's primary booking and discovery channel for guests researching heritage stays in Udaipur.
