Brand Website

SM Trades

Luxury website for an interior decor products brand: wall decor and wallpapers, planters and home accents, in an onyx, espresso and champagne-gold theme.

Interior DecorProduct WebsiteUI/UX DesignAnimation
Visit the live website

The challenge

SM Trades sells interior decorating products and needed a website that looks as refined as the spaces its products go into. The site had to show its range clearly, wall decor and wallpapers, planters and decorative accents, and make it easy for buyers to enquire.

The solution

QivaLabs designed and built a clean, luxury-styled website in an onyx, espresso and champagne-gold palette, with slow, refined animations: line-by-line text reveals, scroll-triggered fades and a scrolling marquee. The product range is organised into clear categories with consistent studio-style product photography, and enquiry paths sit throughout the site. We also connected the custom domain smtradesonline.com and set up hosting on Vercel.
