The challenge

Storaazi wanted a place for shoppers to discover makeup, skincare and personal care products from international brands. It had to be a catalogue, not a store: no prices, discounts, cart or checkout, while still feeling premium and editorial rather than like a template or a generic AI-made site.

The solution

QivaLabs designed and built a fast single-page catalogue with a bold, colour-blocked look, an animated hero banner, a brand ticker and scroll-reveal motion. Visitors can search, filter by category and brand, open detailed product pages with shades, sizes and ingredients, browse a brand directory and explore curated collections. Product information and photography come from each brand's official store, with every product page linking back to its source.

Key features

Search the whole catalogue and narrow it by category and brand, with sorting by name or newest.
