The challenge

Small retailers often bill on paper or basic apps, track stock in notebooks and only learn how the day went when they total the cash. Mistakes in billing, GST and inventory cost money, and most POS vendors ask for a sales call or an install before you can see the product.

The solution

Qiva POS is a cloud-based point of sale and billing system built for shops and small businesses. We published a public, interactive demo so owners can open it in the browser and try billing, products and reports themselves before they commit.

Key features

Retail shops, general stores, boutiques, cafes and small businesses in India looking for affordable POS billing software with inventory management.

🧾

GST billing & invoicing

Create bills and GST-ready invoices quickly at the counter and keep a clean record of every sale.

📦

Inventory management

Track products and stock levels so you know what is selling and when to reorder.

🔍

Barcode & fast checkout

Speed up billing at the counter with quick product search and barcode-based checkout.

💵

Cash, UPI & card payments

Record multiple payment modes against each bill for easy end-of-day reconciliation.

📊

Sales reports & dashboard

See daily sales, top-selling items and trends from a simple dashboard, on any device.

☁️

Cloud-based, works in your browser

Nothing to install: open the POS on a shop computer, tablet or phone and start billing.
