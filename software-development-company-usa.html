The fine print

Contracts, data and compliance for US clients

We sign your NDA before we see anything sensitive, and the contract assigns all intellectual property to your company. The US has no single federal privacy law, so we build to the rules that apply to you: California's CCPA and CPRA if you collect data from California residents, HIPAA safeguards if you handle health information, and PCI DSS rules by keeping card data with Stripe or your payment provider rather than on your servers.

We host in a US region on AWS, Google Cloud, Azure or Vercel, in your account. Accessibility matters too: we design to WCAG 2.2 AA so your site works for every visitor and stands up better to ADA complaints.

FAQ

Questions USA clients ask.

Why hire a team in India instead of a US agency?

Cost is the obvious reason. 2026 rate surveys put senior US developers at about $75 to $150 or more an hour, against roughly $18 to $45 in India. The real test is delivery: ask any offshore team for live work, references and how they handle time zones. We show you all three before you pay anything.

Can you work with our in-house developers?

Yes. We can join your GitHub, Jira or Linear, follow your code review process and work as an extra squad, or take one product end to end.

How do payments work?

Usually a deposit to start, then milestone payments as each part is delivered and approved. We invoice in US dollars.

Do you sign NDAs and assign IP?

Yes. We sign your NDA before the first detailed call, and the contract assigns all code, designs and content to you.
