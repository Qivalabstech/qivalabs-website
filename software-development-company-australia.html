The fine print

Contracts, data and the Privacy Act

If your business is covered by the Privacy Act 1988, the Australian Privacy Principles apply to how personal information is collected, stored and sent overseas. APP 8 makes you responsible for overseas recipients, so we sign a data handling agreement, keep production data in an Australian cloud region in your account, and limit access to the people who need it. We also design so you can meet the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme if something goes wrong.

All intellectual property is assigned to you, we sign your NDA before the first detailed call, and invoices are in Australian dollars.

FAQ

Can you build our app for both iPhone and Android?

Yes. Most clients choose Flutter or React Native, so one codebase runs on both platforms. We also take the app through App Store and Google Play review.

Can our data stay in Australia?

Yes. We set up hosting in the AWS, Azure or Google Cloud Sydney or Melbourne regions, in your account.
