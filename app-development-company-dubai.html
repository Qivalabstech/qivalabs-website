The fine print

Contracts, data and UAE rules

Personal data on the UAE mainland is covered by Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (the PDPL). Companies in the DIFC and ADGM follow their own data protection laws instead. The PDPL's executive regulations had still not been published in 2026, but we build to its principles now: clear consent, limited access and secure hosting, with a UAE cloud region in your account when your data must stay in the country.

The contract assigns all intellectual property to you, we sign your NDA before the first detailed call, and invoices are in AED.
