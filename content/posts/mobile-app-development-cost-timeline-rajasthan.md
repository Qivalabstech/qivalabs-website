Ask five app development companies in Rajasthan to quote "an app for my food delivery business" and the numbers will run from about ₹2 lakh to ₹20 lakh. That spread doesn't mean four of them are lying. They are quoting for different apps, because "an app for my business" can describe anything from a menu that opens on a phone to a three-sided marketplace with live tracking.

We build Android and iOS apps from Udaipur for clients across India, so we see this confusion every week. Here is how we explain it to people before they sign anything.

## What sets the price

Four things decide most of the cost. The first is how many user types the app has. A shop app with only customers is one app. A delivery app has customers, delivery riders and the shop owner, and each of them needs their own screens, permissions and flows. Every extra user type roughly adds another small app to the project.

The second is whether there is a backend. If the app needs logins, saved data, payments or an admin panel, someone has to build and host the server side too. For most business apps, that is around half the work.

The third is real-time features. Live order tracking, chat and instant notifications need extra infrastructure and a lot more testing.

The fourth is the platform choice. A cross-platform framework like Flutter or React Native lets one codebase run on Android and iPhone. That usually saves 30 to 40 percent compared with building two native apps, and for most business apps nobody can tell the difference.

## Typical price ranges in 2026

These are the ranges we see for work done properly in India, including design, backend, testing and store submission:

| Type of app | Typical range | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Simple app, no backend | ₹1.5 lakh to ₹4 lakh | Catalogue, brochure, restaurant menu |
| App with accounts and a basic backend | ₹4 lakh to ₹10 lakh | Booking app, loyalty app, customer portal |
| Marketplace or multi-sided platform | ₹10 lakh to ₹25 lakh | Delivery, services marketplace, ride booking |
| Fintech, healthtech or compliance-heavy | ₹15 lakh and up | Lending, clinic management, insurance |

If a quote lands far below these ranges, ask what has been left out. It is often the admin panel, testing on real devices or the cost of putting the app on the Play Store and App Store.

## How long it takes

A first version that people can use usually takes four to six months:

- Discovery and screen design: 3 to 4 weeks
- Building the backend and the app: 8 to 16 weeks
- Testing and fixing: 2 to 3 weeks
- Store review: about 1 to 2 weeks for Google Play and 1 to 3 weeks for Apple

You can go faster by cutting scope. We often suggest launching on Android first, because most Indian users are on Android, and adding iOS once the idea has proven itself.

## How to choose an app development company in Udaipur

A good team will push back on your idea before they quote it. If a company sends a price after one phone call, they haven't understood what you need. Here is what we would ask any company, including us:

1. Can I see two apps you built that are live on the Play Store right now? Install them and try them.
2. Who owns the source code and the developer accounts when we finish? The answer should be you.
3. What happens after launch? Ask what bug fixes, Android updates and server costs look like in the first year.
4. Will I see working builds as you go, or only at the end? Weekly or fortnightly test builds catch misunderstandings early, while they are still cheap to fix.
5. What is included in the quote? Design, backend, admin panel, testing and store submission should all be listed separately.

Being local helps more than people expect. Sitting with the people who will use the app, whether that's your staff, your riders or your customers, for even one afternoon saves weeks of back and forth later. That's one reason businesses in Udaipur and the rest of Rajasthan like working with a team they can meet.

## What to prepare before you ask for quotes

You don't need a technical document. A one-page note with these points is enough:

- who will use the app, and what each of them needs to do in it
- the three features the first version cannot launch without
- any apps you like, and what you like about them
- your budget range and the date you want to launch

With that, a serious company can give you a written scope and a fixed quote. At QivaLabs we send both within 48 hours of the first conversation. If the app needs to talk to software you already use, such as a billing system, a CRM or WhatsApp, mention it up front. Integrations are where hidden cost usually sits.
