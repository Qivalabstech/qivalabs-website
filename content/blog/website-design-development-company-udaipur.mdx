Udaipur has more web design agencies than most cities its size. That's partly because the barrier to entry is low, and partly because there's real demand from businesses that are serious about their digital presence. The problem is that for a business owner evaluating options, the variation in quality is enormous — and it's not always obvious from the portfolio.

This guide is written from the perspective of a software company that builds websites for clients, but also competes with other agencies for the same work. We'll tell you what actually matters, including the things that would help you pick someone other than us.

## What most businesses get wrong first

The instinct when evaluating web agencies is to look at the portfolio and compare prices. These matter, but they're not the most predictive factors of whether you'll end up with a website that does what you need it to do.

The portfolio tells you whether the agency can produce something that looks good. It tells you very little about:
- Whether those sites load fast on mobile in Indian network conditions
- Whether the site ranks on Google for anything useful
- Whether the client can actually manage their own content
- What happened when something broke six months post-launch
- Whether the conversion rate (visitors → enquiries) is any good

## The questions worth asking

**Who actually builds the site?** Many agencies in Udaipur sub-contract the development work to freelancers or offshore teams, adding their own margin. This isn't always bad, but it affects accountability and support quality significantly. Ask directly: do your developers work in-house?

**What happens after launch?** Some agencies disappear after the final payment. A site isn't done when it's launched — you'll want to make content changes, fix bugs, and improve pages based on what your visitors actually do. What does their post-launch support look like, and what does it cost?

**Can you see the Google Search Console data for one of their client sites?** Anyone can produce a pretty-looking website. A site that actually ranks on Google for relevant search terms — that's harder. If an agency claims SEO expertise, ask them to show you what organic traffic growth looks like for a client site. If they can't or won't, take that for what it is.

**What technology do they build on, and why?** WordPress with a page builder theme is fine for many businesses, but it comes with real tradeoffs — ongoing plugin updates, security vulnerabilities, and often poor performance. Modern frameworks like Next.js produce faster, more maintainable sites. Neither is universally right or wrong; the question is whether the agency can tell you why they're recommending what they're recommending.

## What a good web development engagement looks like

A quality project should start with questions, not designs. Before any mockup is produced, the agency should understand your business, your competitors, your target customers in Udaipur and/or across India, what you want visitors to do when they land on the site, and what your existing conversion problem is (or isn't).

Design should follow content strategy, not precede it. Agencies that show you beautiful designs before thinking about what the pages actually say are optimising for a wow moment in a sales call, not for a site that converts.

The development phase should produce a site that's genuinely fast — a Lighthouse performance score above 90, images in WebP/AVIF format, no render-blocking JavaScript, and a Core Web Vitals pass on real devices. These aren't cosmetic details; they're direct ranking factors for Google.

## What to expect to pay in Udaipur

A basic corporate website with 5–8 pages, built on WordPress or similar: ₹30,000–₹70,000. You're mainly paying for design customisation and setup.

A custom-built website with original design, built on Next.js or similar modern stack, with SEO setup, fast loading, and a CMS for content management: ₹80,000–₹2,00,000. This is what mid-size businesses should budget for if the website is a meaningful part of their customer acquisition.

A fully custom web application with booking, payments, user accounts, or complex integrations: ₹2,50,000+, and you should be evaluating against the software development criteria in [our cost guide](/blog/custom-software-development-cost-india-2026).

## Red flags

An agency that can't explain why a particular site they built ranks on Google is not an SEO agency — they're a design agency calling themselves one. Being honest about this distinction matters.

Offers to build a website in 3–5 days almost always mean a template with minimal customisation. This can be fine for some use cases, but you should know that's what you're getting.

Lock-in on proprietary builders means you can't easily move to another provider later. Prefer agencies that build on standard, portable platforms.
