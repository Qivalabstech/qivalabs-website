The fine print

Contracts, data and GDPR

Personal data of people in the EU is covered by the GDPR. India has no EU adequacy decision, so when our team needs access we sign the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses alongside a data processing agreement. Production data stays in an EU region such as Frankfurt, Dublin or Amsterdam, in your cloud account.

The European Accessibility Act has applied since 28 June 2025 to many online shops and digital services, so we design and test to WCAG 2.2 AA. All intellectual property is assigned to you, and invoices are in euros.

FAQ

Questions Europe clients ask.

Which European countries do you work with?

Any of them. Most enquiries come from Germany, the Netherlands, Ireland and France, and we work in English.

How do you handle GDPR when the team is in India?

We sign a data processing agreement and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, keep production data in an EU region, and limit access to named people.

Can you build multilingual sites and stores?

Yes. We set up language and currency handling from the start, with translations you or your agency supply.

How is this different from a nearshore team in Eastern Europe?

Eastern European rates in 2026 run roughly $45 to $90 an hour for senior developers, India about $18 to $45. You lose an hour or two of overlap. We suggest comparing fixed quotes for the same scope.
