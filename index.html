FAQ

Questions people ask us.

What does QivaLabs do?

QivaLabs is a software, app and AI development company based in India. We design and build custom software, mobile apps, websites, AI agents and automation for startups and businesses in the USA, UK, Australia, Europe and the UAE, and we maintain them after launch.

Do you work with clients in the US, UK, Australia, Europe and Dubai?

Yes, that is our main focus. Brands in the United States such as Wee Care Solutions in Texas and Stashfin run on sites we built. Projects run fully online, with calls in your working hours, quotes in your currency, your NDA signed before the first detailed call, and all code and IP assigned to you.

How do you handle time zones?

We are on India time (UTC+5:30). That gives near-full overlap with Dubai, four to five hours with the UK and Europe, your afternoon in Australia, and your morning in the US. Written updates and demo links arrive before your next working day.

How quickly will I get a quote?

Send us a short brief and you get a written scope, a timeline and a fixed quote within 48 hours. There is no obligation.

Can you build an ERP or billing system for a small business?

Yes. We build ERP, CRM, inventory and billing software sized for small and mid-sized businesses, and we can start from a ready product like Qiva POS when that saves you money.

Do you offer support after the website or app goes live?

Yes. We offer maintenance plans (AMC), 24/7 technical support and a named account manager, so the people who built your system also look after it.
