The fine print

Contracts, data and UK GDPR

Personal data of UK residents falls under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, and cookies and marketing emails under PECR. India does not have a UK adequacy decision, so when our team needs access to personal data we sign the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Better still, the data itself stays in a UK region such as AWS London, in your account.

The contract assigns all intellectual property to you, we sign your NDA before the first detailed call, and we invoice in pounds.

FAQ

Questions UK clients ask.

Can you work as a white-label partner for our agency?

Yes, it is one of the most common ways UK agencies work with us. You stay the client contact, and we design and build under your brand.

How do you handle UK GDPR?

We sign a data processing agreement, keep production data in a UK or EU region, limit who can access it, and use the ICO transfer agreement for any access from India.

How much cheaper is this than a UK agency?

Rate surveys for 2026 put UK and Western European developers at roughly $60 to $120 an hour, and Indian teams at about $18 to $45. Your saving depends on the project, so we give you a fixed quote you can compare like for like.

Will we have the same people throughout?

Yes. The team on your discovery call stays on the project.
